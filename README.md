# LeitnerBoxes

[Open LeitnerBoxes](https://thechrisbellman.github.io/LeitnerBoxes/)

A browser-based French workplace vocabulary practice app using a five-box Leitner review system.

## About

LeitnerBoxes is an unofficial study aid. Its units follow the archived Government of Canada PFL2 language-training materials:

- [PFL2 A/B archive](https://publications.aws.tpsgc-pwgsc.cloud-nuage.canada.ca/site/fra/recherche/CataloguedesproduitsdeformationlinguistiqueAB.html)
- [PFL2 C archive catalogue](https://publications.gc.ca/site/eng/search/LanguageLearningProductsCatalogueC.html?wbdisable=false)

The app is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Government of Canada. Government source material remains subject to the [Canada.ca terms and conditions](https://www.canada.ca/en/transparency/terms.html?lang=en).

## Privacy

No accounts or backend. Progress stays in your browser. See [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md).
